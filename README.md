# Liu W.

I help small online sellers with three kinds of fixed-scope work:

- **Product photos → short vertical ad videos.** Zoom and pan motion, captions, a call to action, optional music. Your photos are not redrawn or altered. See [product-photo-ad-video](https://github.com/awin0377/product-photo-ad-video).
